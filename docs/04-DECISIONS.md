# Decision log

Includes things tried and rejected. The rejections carry as much information as
the decisions — do not re-litigate them without new evidence.

---

## D1. Client mod, not server mod — SETTLED

SPT mods split into server mods (`user\mods`, C# since SPT 4.0, handle profiles /
traders / quests / items / bot spawning) and client mods (`BepInEx\plugins`, C#,
patch the game directly).

Aiming is in-raid mechanics, which server mods cannot touch. **Client mod.**

---

## D2. Scope narrowed from "bodycam mod" to "free aim" — SETTLED

Initial framing was a full Bodycam recreation: chest camera, no sight alignment,
no ADS zoom, decoupled motion, wide-angle video look.

Owner corrected: *"I don't need an actual body camera, just gun aiming decoupled
from my eye view."*

**Dropped:** chest camera, lens distortion, vignette, grain, video overlay, removing
sight alignment.

**Kept:** the decoupling mechanic only.

---

## D3. Deadzone IS the Bodycam mechanic — SETTLED, after an initial wrong call

Claude initially guessed Bodycam's feel came from sway, camera smoothing and lens
work, **not** deadzone. That was wrong. Evidence that settled it:

- The most-asked question about Bodycam's feel, on r/UnrealEngine5, is phrased
  *"the gun moves around the screen in your hands without turning camera"* — the
  answer given is deadzone aiming, with Insurgency Sandstorm as the reference
- Unreal tutorials for recreating the Bodycam/Unrecord look are titled
  *"How to make a deadzone aim (Free aim)"*
- A Steam Workshop mod "Free Aim & Bodycam System" describes itself as decoupled
  aiming within a deadzone before the camera turns
- A Left 4 Dead 2 collection describes itself as a free-aim experience
  *"inspired by Bodycam and Unrecord"*

**Why the initial guess was wrong:** Claude over-read three weak signals — an Arma 3
mod describing itself as camera tweaks, a review naming "sway", and a player
complaining the aim felt disconnected from the mouse. That last one was read
backwards: "not connected to my mouse" is what deadzone feels like to someone
expecting normal FPS aiming, not evidence against it.

---

## D4. Gun-leads-camera-chases, NOT camera-leads-gun-trails — SETTLED by measurement

The pivotal finding. Established by the owner testing Bodycam directly.

| | lualeet's SPT mod | Bodycam |
|---|---|---|
| Mouse drives | The camera | The gun |
| The other one | Gun dragged behind | Camera springs to follow |
| Offset behaviour | Stays where pushed, never returns | Always converges to zero in ~1s |
| Feel | Gun heavy, mouse connected to view | Gun crisp, body heavy |

**Consequence:** `lualeet/sptarkov-deadzone` is no longer a usable base for the
drive loop. Only its pivot-rotation maths survives, for the apply step.

**Consequence:** effort estimate went from 18–28 hours to 30–50 hours. The increase
is real — intercepting input and decoupling recoil are both new work with no
existing code to lean on.

---

## D5. "Lag" was the wrong frame — SETTLED

An early version of the plan treated weapon lag as a feature worth copying. The
owner pushed back: *"why lag, does the bodycam have the lag?"*

Correct answer: Bodycam has **no** gun lag. The gun is instant. The weight is all
in the body.

**But** note the subtlety: in lualeet's reactive design the damping is not
decoration, it is the mechanism. The offset is generated by comparing raw camera
rotation to a smoothed copy, so setting damping to zero means no deadzone at all.
That design cannot have its lag removed without losing the feature. This is another
reason the reactive design is the wrong base.

---

## D6. Stance gating — ADOPTED (owner's idea)

Free aim only when the weapon is up and ready. Gun down or slung means normal
Tarkov camera.

Rationale: free aim is a nuisance while looting. Gating it to the ready position
means it only bites in a fight.

**Do not depend on the Realism mod at runtime** to get this. Realism has an
`EStance` enum (`None`, `LowReady`, `HighReady`, `ShortStock`, `ActiveAiming`,
`PatrolStance`, `Melee`, `PistolCompressed`) with `PatrolStance` being exactly the
gun-down state — but it also rewrites ballistics, medical and recoil, so users
would be forced to take all of it. Read the source, build your own toggle.

## D7. Realism already ported the pivot maths to current SPT — USEFUL FINDING

`space-commits/SPT-Realism-Mod-Client`, `Player/StanceController.cs`, contains a
comment crediting lualeet's deadzone mod, followed by the same rotation maths
working on current SPT for Realism's weapon mounting system.

This de-risks the port: same member names (`HandsContainer.WeaponRootAnim`,
`MovementContext.Yaw`/`.Pitch`, `LocalRotateAround`), known working on 4.1.5.

See `reference/realism-StanceController-excerpt.cs`.

---

## D8. Shouldering animation out of scope for v1 — SETTLED

The three-phase mount observed in Bodycam (buttstock connect, cant correction,
stabilisation settle, plus occasional re-grip) is bespoke animation authoring, not
a Harmony patch. Different discipline, much longer project. Ship the aiming model
first.

---

## D9. Both-eyes-open optics: build Tier 1, spike Tier 2, skip Tier 3 — RECOMMENDED

See `01-SPEC.md` section 6 for the tiers.

Reasoning: the screen-space reticle (Tier 1) is nearly free once free aim exists
and delivers the actual playing benefit. Ghosting the housing (Tier 2) is the
visual payoff and worth a timeboxed spike, but carries three specific risks.
Near-field blur (Tier 3) is polish for screenshots and contributes almost nothing
to how the game plays.

---

## D10. Reactive design is the documented fallback — STANDING

If step 03 (intercepting the look input) proves infeasible from a client mod, fall
back to lualeet's reactive approach. It feels different — gun heavy instead of body
heavy — but it works and the code exists. Both approaches are used in Bodycam-like
games, so the fallback is not un-authentic.

---

## D11. Compensate mode is the default drive loop — NEW

The planned design (intercept the mouse delta before it reaches
`MovementContext`) is retained as Intercept mode, but is no longer the only way
to get the coupling. Compensate mode reaches the same feel without intercepting
anything, at the cost of the character's movement bearing tracking the gun
rather than the view.

Both run the identical drive loop. Switching is one config value.

Full reasoning and the tradeoffs in `07-FINDINGS.md` F4. This does not overturn
D4 - it depends on it.

---

## D12. Push factor is 0.5, not 1.0 — NEW, CORRECTS THE SPEC

The spec's pseudocode implies a push factor of 1.0, at which the soft cone
becomes a hard clamp and a flick can never reach the measured ~90 degrees. Below
1.0 all three measured behaviours hold at once.

Derivation and test output in `07-FINDINGS.md` F3.

---

## Open risk: Battlestate's own aiming rework — CLOSED

Battlestate previewed "realistic non-linear aiming" changes in March 2026, to be
tested on their Early Test Server first. Community reception was poor and it could
not be confirmed whether it shipped. If it landed and flows into a future SPT
release, part of this project may become redundant.

**Resolved.** Still on test servers as of September 2026; live is 1.1.0.0 without
it. See `07-FINDINGS.md` F2.

**Replaced by a different standing risk:** SPT itself changed hands in August
2026 after a trademark notice from Battlestate. The project continues as Single
Player Tushonka under a new maintainer. Version churn is now less predictable.
See `07-FINDINGS.md` F1.
